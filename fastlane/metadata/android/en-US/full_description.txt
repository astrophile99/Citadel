Citadel is a task app that doesn't feel like one.

Every day you name a few real things you mean to do — study, a walk, a phone call you've been putting off. Each becomes a mission. Keep one, and your Citadel answers: a lantern lights along the wall, the windows warm, the campfire grows. Keep them for weeks, and the kingdom slowly comes to life — flowers by the walls, birds on the roofs, one sleepy hound by the fire.

That is the whole reward. No points, no badges, no streaks to lose.

A PLACE, NOT A LIST
The world is the app. It follows the real time of day — mist at dawn, long light in the afternoon, a warm horizon at dusk, stars at night — and your missions sit on its lower ground rather than beneath a picture of it.

NO GUILT, EVER
• Unfinished missions are never "overdue" or "failed". They rest in the Chronicle, ready to continue whenever you like.
• Time away brings a little mist over the valley — never ruin. Everything you've built stays built.
• There is no streak counter, because one bad week shouldn't erase a good month.

A SHORT RITUAL EACH EVENING
Before sleep, prepare tomorrow's missions. Around five is a good night's plan; fewer is also a plan. In the morning, the gates are already open.

WHISPERS THROUGH THE DAY
If you'd like, the kingdom can send a few quiet notifications — at dawn to plan the day, at midday, afternoon and evening to follow through, and at night to prepare tomorrow. Choose five, four or two a day, move them to your own hours, or turn them off. They never nag, they replace each other instead of piling up, and they stay silent while you're already in the app.

A WINDOW ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
The Citadel widget shows your kingdom at this hour and the promises you've set for today, drawn by the same hand as the app.

YOURS ALONE
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking — Citadel doesn't even ask for internet access.

Also:
• A five-page guide on first launch, and any time from the Sanctuary
• Weighted missions — a Skirmish, a Fortification or an Expedition — so the big things move the world more
• Daily missions that renew themselves each morning
• Respects your system's "remove animations" setting
• Built for dark rooms: readable, calm, gold only where it matters

Citadel is for anyone who has quit a productivity app because it made them feel behind. Open it, see what matters today, then close it and go live your day. The kingdom will be here when you come back.
