Citadel is a calm to-do app with a little castle.

Each day, add a few real things you want to do: study, a walk, that phone call you keep putting off. When you finish one, tap it and a lantern lights up on the castle wall. The windows glow and the fire grows. Keep going for a few weeks and the castle comes to life, with flowers by the walls, birds on the roofs and a sleepy dog by the fire.

That's the whole reward. No points, no badges, no streaks to lose.

YOUR DAY, AS A PLACE
The castle follows the real time of day: misty mornings, long afternoons, warm sunsets and starry nights. Your tasks sit right on top of it.

NO GUILT, EVER
• Unfinished tasks are never "overdue". They wait in History, and you can bring them back whenever you like.
• Time away only brings a little mist. Nothing you build is ever lost.
• There's no streak counter, because one bad week shouldn't erase a good month.

SIMPLE TASK CONTROLS
Every task has three clear buttons: Done, Skip and Delete. Skipped tasks wait in History for another day.

PLAN TOMORROW IN A MINUTE
Before bed, add tomorrow's tasks. About five is a good plan; fewer is fine too.

FRIENDLY REMINDERS
If you like, Citadel can send a few gentle reminders: in the morning to plan, at midday, afternoon and evening to check in, and at night to plan tomorrow. Choose five, four or two a day, set your own times, or turn them off. They never nag, they replace each other instead of piling up, and they stay quiet while you're using the app.

A HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
See your castle and today's tasks right on your home screen.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Citadel doesn't even ask for internet access.

Also:
• A short guide on first launch, and any time from Settings
• Task sizes (Small, Medium, Big), so bigger tasks light up the castle more
• Daily tasks that come back fresh every morning
• Respects your phone's "remove animations" setting
• Easy on the eyes in dark rooms

Citadel is for anyone who has given up on a to-do app because it made them feel behind. Open it, see what matters today, then go live your day. Your castle will be here when you come back.
