# Citadel Reward System: Celebrating Consistency

## The Reward Paradigm: Visual Abundance
Citadel does not reward the Commander with numbers, level-ups, gold coins, or badges. These gamified systems trigger cheap dopamine hits that quickly fade and lead to habit fatigue. 

Instead, the reward in Citadel is the **flourishing of the world itself**.

---

## Rewards in Citadel

### 1. Campfire and Lighting intensity
*   **Action**: Completed missions.
*   **Reward**: The campfire burns higher, throwing dynamic, warm shadows across the camp. Lanterns are lit along the wall. The dark world feels warmer and safer.

### 2. Environmental Life
*   **Action**: Weekly consistency.
*   **Reward**: The world wakes up:
    *   *Week 1*: Small green sprouts appear near the walls.
    *   *Week 2*: White mountain flowers bloom.
    *   *Week 3*: Small forest birds land on the roofs.
    *   *Week 4*: A dog wanders near the campfire, sleeping peacefully near the flames.

### 3. The Hearth Log (Journal)
*   **Action**: Ending the day.
*   **Reward**: The Companion writes a short, poetic entry in the *Hearth Log* (e.g., *"A quiet night. The fire was bright, and the wind was still. The walls stood strong."*). This provides a sense of permanent, comfortable history that the Commander can read through.
