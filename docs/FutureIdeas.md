# Citadel: Future Horizons

## Long-Term Aspirations
These ideas represent features that enrich the atmosphere, depth, and community feelings of the Citadel, but are held back to keep Version 1 focused and polished.

---

## 1. Dynamic Seasons & Weather Engine
*   **Concept**: Integrate a local weather API (or cycle weather conditions internally based on completion streaks) so the Citadel matches the outdoor environment of the Commander.
*   **Visual impact**: Rain falling down the screen, wind blowing autumn leaves, or gentle snow gathering on roofs during winter nights.

---

## 2. The Traveler's Hearth (Community)
*   **Concept**: A peaceful, non-intrusive way to interact with other players.
*   **Gameplay**: Travelers (other players) occasionally stop by the Commander's campfire. They do not talk or message. They simply sit at the fire for a day. Clicking them reveals a short, anonymous diary entry written by that player (*"Commander Lyra of the southern hills spent today writing music. The forest is quiet."*). This provides a sense of shared human warmth without social media noise or toxicity.

---

## 3. The Chronicle Archives
*   **Concept**: An interactive journal containing the historical records of the Citadel.
*   **Gameplay**: The Commander can open a beautifully rendered chronicle book to scroll through past dates. Each page shows the Citadel's status on that day, weather, active companion dialogue, and which promises were completed. It serves as a personal memoir of growth.

---

## 4. The Citadel Soundtrack
*   **Concept**: Atmospheric, minimal acoustic instrument arrangements (acoustic guitar, piano, woodwinds) that change tempo and tone based on the time of day and the status of the kingdom.
*   **Mood**: Sleepy, acoustic ambient tunes that enhance the sense of home and rest.
