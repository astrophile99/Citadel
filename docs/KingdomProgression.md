# Citadel Progression: Growing without Pressure

## Progression Philosophy
We reject linear progression models where the player "levels up" from a wooden hut to a stone castle. This creates an unhealthy expectation of permanent growth and turns self-care into a grind. 

Instead, progression in Citadel is **restorative, seasonal, and circular**.

---

## The "Wild Nature" System
We do not use structural decay (e.g., walls crumbling or fire destroying buildings). That would induce anxiety. Instead, we use the natural progression of wild nature:

*   **The Wild**: When the Commander is away, nature gently reclaims the Citadel. Vines crawl up the stone walls, leaves gather in the courtyard, and mist settles in.
*   **The Awakening**: Returning is an act of clearing the wild. Completing missions trims the vines, sweeps the leaves, and opens up the landscape.
*   *Why this works*: It feels like returning to a summer cottage that has been closed for winter. It is an act of tidying up a cozy, familiar home, not recovering from a disaster.

---

## Long-term Cycles: Seasons & Weather
The Citadel experiences its own calendar, which aligns with real-world seasons and weather (or cycles internally over weeks):

1.  **Spring/Green Season**: Meadows are green, flowers sprout, and rain clears the fog.
2.  **Autumn/Golden Season**: The leaves turn warm orange, falling softly across the screen. Fires look warmer.
3.  **Winter/Silent Season**: Soft snow falls. The campfire glows with high contrast against the snow-covered roofs.
4.  **Weather States**: Overcast, misty, rainy, clear, and starlit. Weather matches the emotional state of the Citadel, providing natural visual variety that feels organic and handcrafted.
