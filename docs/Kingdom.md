# The Citadel: The Living Kingdom

## The Centerpiece
The Citadel is the visual and emotional anchor of the application. It is represented by a stylized, minimalist illustration positioned at the top of the main screen. 

The Citadel is not a static game screen, nor is it a complex 3D rendering. It is a living silhouette that reacts dynamically to the Commander's life.

---

## Metaphors in the Landscape

### 1. The Watchtower Fire (The Campfire)
*   **Represents**: Daily activity and current momentum.
*   **State Shifts**: 
    *   *High Consistency*: A bright, warm gold flame flickering with tiny spark particles floating upward.
    *   *Away / Rest*: A low, dim orange ember glowing softly in the dark.
    *   *Return*: Sparks flare up as the first mission is checked.

### 2. The Mist & Clouds
*   **Represents**: Fatigue, absence, or pause.
*   **State Shifts**: 
    *   If no missions have been completed for 2+ days, a layer of soft, dark mist rolls across the bottom of the Citadel silhouette.
    *   Completing a mission starts to blow the mist away, restoring visibility to the meadows.

### 3. The Lanterns
*   **Represents**: Accomplished promises.
*   **State Shifts**: Each completed mission lights a small hanging lantern along the Citadel walls. The light is warm gold and pulses gently.

### 4. Ambient Life
*   **Represents**: Long-term consistency.
*   **State Shifts**: 
    *   **Birds**: Gather on the watchtower roof when tasks are completed early in the day.
    *   **Smoke**: Gently curls from the chimneys when the Commander is active, showing the town is inhabited.
    *   **Gardens**: Small green sprouts appear near the walls, turning into silent white flowers as consistency builds over weeks.
