# Citadel Notifications: Whispers from the Kingdom

## Objective
Notifications in Citadel are not reminders designed to force clicks. They are small story beats—quiet messages from the kingdom that evoke curiosity and invite the Commander home.

---

## Tone Guidelines
*   **Never demand**: Do not use words like "must," "urgent," "forgot," "streak," or "overdue."
*   **Never shame**: Do not highlight missed actions.
*   **Use world terminology**: Talk about the hearth, the gates, the watchtowers, the gardens, the scouts.
*   **Evoke atmosphere**: Focus on time of day, weather, or natural events in the Citadel.

---

## Notification Writing Reference

### 🌅 Morning (Dawn has arrived)
*   *"Dawn has arrived, Commander. The mist is rising off the meadows."*
*   *"The scouts have returned with today’s paths. The gates are open."*
*   *"A fresh sunrise, Commander. The hearth awaits your spark."*

### 🌆 Evening (Dusk/Night)
*   *"The lanterns are lit along the eastern wall. Rest easy tonight."*
*   *"The fire is stoked for the evening, Commander. Join us when you are ready."*
*   *"The stars are clear over the Citadel tonight. The watch is set."*

### 🍂 Seasonal / Special
*   *"The first autumn leaves have drifted into the courtyard. The air is cool."*
*   *"A quiet rain falls over the gardens. The flowers are waking."*
*   *"The gates recognize your footsteps, Commander. Welcome home."*
