# Citadel Companion: The Quiet Guardian

## The Character
The Companion is not an assistant, a cute mascot, or a chatbot. It is a quiet guardian of the Citadel—an old friend who has watched over the gates for generations. 

*   **Tone**: Wise, gentle, calm, patient, occasionally humorous.
*   **Speech**: Sparse, meaningful.
*   **Presence**: Quiet, physical, integrated into the environment.

---

## Behavior & Non-Verbal Presence
The Companion lives directly within the main silhouette illustration. Rather than popping up with text alerts, they communicate presence through where they stand and how they move:

*   🌅 **Morning**: Sitting on a bench near the cold fire, holding a hot clay mug.
*   ☀️ **Afternoon**: Leaning against a wooden pillar near the gates, watching the road.
*   🌆 **Evening**: Stoking the campfire, wrapped in a blanket.
*   🌙 **Night**: Standing on the parapet wall, holding a staff with a dim gold lantern, gazing at the stars.

Tapping the Companion does not open a settings menu. It triggers a short, warm, time-specific observation.

---

## Companion Dialogue Guidelines

*   **Keep it brief**: Max 1-2 lines.
*   **No tech jargon**: They never speak of "databases," "notifications," "tasks," or "streaks."
*   **Acknowledge returning with warmth**:
    *   *Bad*: "Welcome back! You haven't checked in for 5 days. Let's finish your tasks!"
    *   *Good*: "The road was long, Commander. Sit near the hearth. The tea is fresh."
*   **Acknowledge evening rest**:
    *   *Good*: "The watch is set for tonight, Commander. Rest your mind. Dawn will find us ready."
