# Citadel Anti-Goals: What We Will Never Become

To maintain Citadel’s soul, we establish clear design boundaries. These anti-goals represent common industry patterns that Citadel **intentionally and permanently rejects**.

---

## 1. No Guilt-Driven Productivity
*   We will never track or display "overdue" counts on the launcher icon or dashboard.
*   We will never warn the user about what they missed or how long they have been away.
*   The Companion will never make a sarcastic, nagging, or disappointed comment.

## 2. No Streak Anxiety
*   We will never show a "streak number" (e.g., 🔥 15 Days). Streaks create a fragile habit loop that collapses into shame once broken.
*   We reward the act of returning, not the performance of perfect continuity.

## 3. No Manipulative Notifications
*   We will never send notifications to drive user retention metrics (DAU/MAU).
*   Notifications must never use false urgency ("Hurry up," "Only 2 hours left!").
*   If the user disables notifications, the app functions perfectly and never nags them to re-enable them.

## 4. No Addictive Loops
*   No slot-machine checkmark sounds.
*   No loot boxes, randomized currencies, or daily log-in streaks.
*   The app does not want to keep the user scrolling. We measure success by how quickly the user exits the app to live their real life.

## 5. No Feature Bloat
*   No subtasks, tags, pomodoro timers, calendar integrations, project boards, or team collaboration systems.
*   We choose extreme polish of a few simple tools over a large feature list.

## 6. No Social Comparison
*   No leaderboards, community feed scrolls, or peer-pressure metrics.
*   The Commander's journey is deeply personal and confidential.

## 7. No Dark UX Patterns
*   No hidden buttons or complex flows to clear tasks.
*   No pushy requests for ratings, reviews, or signups.
