# Citadel: Version 1 Scope

## Core Target
Version 1 must focus entirely on polishing the **core emotional loop**. We will not build complex customization options, community features, or data exports. We want the initial user (the Commander) to feel the serenity of returning to their Citadel.

---

## Included Features

1.  **Time-Aware Theme**:
    *   Dawn, Day, Dusk, and Night background shifts.
    *   Dynamic status bar and navigation bar coloring.
2.  **The Citadel Silhouette (V1 Centerpiece)**:
    *   A static, beautiful vector illustration that reflects the time of day.
    *   Campfire that burns brighter on completed missions and turns to low orange embers at night/absence.
    *   Mist layer that rolls in if no tasks are completed for 2+ days.
3.  **Clean-Slate Mission Management**:
    *   Create daily missions with semantic difficulty (Minor, Moderate, Major).
    *   Mark missions complete with a fading visual transition.
    *   The "Midnight Sweep": Uncompleted missions are automatically archived at midnight, starting the next day clean.
4.  **Companion Presence (V1)**:
    *   Companion icon/character sitting near the campfire or standing on watch depending on time of day.
    *   Tapping the companion displays a welcoming, poetic, time-sensitive piece of guidance.
5.  **Warm Return Experience**:
    *   If the Commander has been gone for over 36 hours, show a dedicated greeting card with comforting dialogue from the Companion.

---

## Out of Scope (For Future Versions)
*   *Seasons and dynamic weather* (snow/rain).
*   *NPC villagers, market stalls, and dogs*.
*   *Custom silhouette building layouts*.
*   *Cloud sync / multi-device sign-in*. (Keep data purely local in SQLite/Room).
